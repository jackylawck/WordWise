## English Version

### 1. Zero Data Retention & Privacy by Design (GDPR Compliant)
WordWise is engineered based on the **Privacy by Design** principle under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). 
* **100% Local Processing:** All text processing, character counts, and conversions (OpenCC) are executed strictly within your browser (client-side).
* **Zero Server Transmission:** No data is transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any remote server.
* **No Cookies & Analytics:** We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party behavioral trackers.

### 2. Artificial Intelligence & Ethics Disclosure (EU AI Act & ISO/IEC 42001)
* **Rule-Based System (Non-Generative AI):** WordWise utilizes rule-based algorithms (regular expressions and rule-based dictionaries via OpenCC) for text conversion and counting. It does not employ generative artificial intelligence or automated profiling engines.
* **Minimal Risk Architecture:** In compliance with the EU AI Act classification framework, this application presents Minimal Risk to user rights, autonomy, and security.
* **AI Training Opt-Out Guarantee:** Since no input text leaves your local machine, your data will **never** be harvested or utilized for training artificial intelligence models (e.g., LLMs).

### 3. Security & ISO Compliance Standards (ISO/IEC 27001)
* **Supply Chain Security:** Zero-dependency runtime architecture eliminating third-party Node.js supply-chain vulnerabilities.
* **Content Security Policy (CSP):** Strict CSP headers implemented to prevent Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) and unauthorized external script execution.

## 中文版本

### 1. 零資料留存與隱私設計 (符合 GDPR 規範)
「智識計 | WordWise」遵循歐盟通用數據保護條例 (GDPR) 的 **Privacy by Design (預設隱私設計)** 原則：
* **100% 本地運算：** 所有文字統計、格式整理及 OpenCC 繁簡轉換均嚴格在您的瀏覽器（用戶端）內執行。
* **零伺服器傳輸：** 任何貼入的文字內容均不會上傳、儲存或傳輸至任何遠端伺服器。
* **無 Cookie 與追蹤器：** 本工具不使用任何 Tracking Cookies、數據分析腳本或第三方行為追蹤器。

### 2. 人工智慧與倫理揭露 (符合 歐盟 AI 法案與 ISO/IEC 42001)
* **規則型系統（非生成式 AI）：** 本工具採用確定性規則演算法（正則表達式與 OpenCC 規則字典）進行轉換與統計，不包含生成式 AI 或自動化個人化決策引擎。
* **極低風險分類：** 依據歐盟 AI 法案 (EU AI Act) 風險劃分，本應用對使用者權益與資訊安全屬於「極低風險 (Minimal Risk)」等級。
* **拒絕 AI 模型訓練保障：** 由於資料絕不離開您的裝置，您的敏感文案或公司機密文件**絕不可能**被收集或用於訓練 AI 大語言模型 (LLMs)。

### 3. 資訊安全與 ISO 標準 (符合 ISO/IEC 27001)
* **供應鏈安全：** 採用零依賴運行架構，防範 npm / Node.js 軟體供應鏈安全漏洞。
* **內容安全策略 (CSP)：** 實施嚴格的 CSP 安全標頭，防禦跨站腳本攻擊 (XSS) 及未授權的外部資源調用。
